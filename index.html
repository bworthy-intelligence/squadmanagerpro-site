An assistant coach who never misses training

  • Session plans built for your squad, not templates. The AI reads your development pathway, your playing philosophy and your recent sessions, then plans what this squad needs next.
  • Match talks in your team's voice. Pre-game team talks and individual briefings that use your own philosophy's language, ready to deliver from your phone.
  • Post-match analysis that reads your notes, not just the score. What worked, what to train next, and how each player is tracking.
  • A presentation night speech kit. At season's end, a season story plus a speakable paragraph for every player. Stand up, read it out, watch the room.

Match day, sorted

  • Fixtures import themselves from league platforms like Dribl.
  • Formations and line-ups in seconds, with fair minutes visible at a glance.
  • Record the match live: goals, assists, subs and key moments, synced to the match clock.
  • Parents follow live from anywhere, so nobody misses a goal because of a work trip.
  • Stats and leaderboards across the season, and the seasons before it.

Your club's coaching, owned by the club

Your club's coaching philosophy, methodology and development pathways should be assets the club owns, not knowledge that walks out the door when a coach moves on. The Training Console gives club admins a central command centre: a shared drill library, a structured training plan repository, and visibility across every squad. Push training plans to any squad or the whole club, standardise your methodology from grassroots up, and when coaches change, your IP stays.
