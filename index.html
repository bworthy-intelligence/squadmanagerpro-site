For coaches

Everything a season asks of you, sorted

Get serious about player development

A proper development pathway for every age band, from first-season MiniRoos to competitive juniors: what to teach, when to teach it, and the drills to teach it with. Watch each topic go from first introduced to second nature as the season unfolds.

Session plans in minutes, not evenings

Pick what you want to work on and your assistant coach builds the full session: warm-up to cool-down, timings, coaching points and diagrams. Not sure what to work on? Let the app recommend a focus, based on your playing philosophy, your development pathway and your recent training and match notes.

Match day, handled

Fixtures import themselves from league platforms like Dribl. Line-ups take seconds, with everyone's game time visible at a glance. Record goals, assists and subs as they happen: the stats that matter to your team keep themselves, and parents follow along live from anywhere.

A season worth talking about

Pre-game talks in your team's own voice. Post-match analysis that reads your notes, not just the scoreline. And when the season wraps up, a presentation night speech kit: the story of your season, with a paragraph worth reading aloud for every single player.

For clubs

For club administrators and technical directors

Your club's coaching philosophy, methodology and development pathways should be assets the club owns, not knowledge that walks out the door when a coach moves on. The Training Console gives your club a central command centre: a shared drill library, a structured training plan repository, and visibility across every squad and age group. Push training plans to any squad or the whole club, standardise your methodology from MiniRoos up, and when coaches change, your IP stays.

Club licences from $199 per year for a 10 coach club

Scaling with your number of coaches, and including a club administrator or technical director licence alongside the coach seats, as well as a generous allocation of supporter licences. Around $20 to $25 a coach, instead of $30 going it alone, and the more coaches you bring on board, the better the per-coach rate.
