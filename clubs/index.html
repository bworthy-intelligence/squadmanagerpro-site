SquadManager Pro for clubs

For club administrators and technical directors: one licence, every coach equipped, and your club's coaching methodology retained when volunteers move on. Built for community football clubs serious about player development.

1. Every coach gets the full kit

  • Session planning built on a proper development curriculum, tuned to each squad's age and level, with an AI assistant doing the heavy lifting
  • Nearly 100 curated drills with step-by-step diagrams a first-time coach can teach
  • Pre-match talks, post-match analysis and an end-of-season presentation speech kit
  • Fixture import, line-ups, live match recording, and live matches parents can follow from anywhere

2. The club keeps its IP

The Training Console gives your club a central command centre: a shared drill library, a structured training plan repository, and visibility across every squad and age group. Push training plans to any squad or the whole club and standardise your methodology from grassroots up. When coaches change, the methodology stays with the club.

3. Simple pricing

From $199 per year for a 10 coach club

Scaling with your number of coaches, and including a club administrator or technical director licence, as well as a generous allocation of supporter licences. Around $20 to $25 a coach, instead of $30 going it alone, and the more coaches you bring on board, the better the per-coach rate.
